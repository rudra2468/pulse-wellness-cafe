About PULSE

Healthy, done right.

Most cafés put indulgence first, and most nutrition apps only tell you what to eat. PULSE does both jobs: it recommends the right drink for your goal and makes it fresh in an open kitchen.

Our vision

To become India’s most trusted wellness café brand, where every drink is fresh, transparent and matched to the person drinking it.

Our mission

To make healthy eating and drinking as appealing, convenient and social as a regular café visit, and to use AI to remove the guesswork of matching food to a personal health goal.

The PULSE AI nutrition engine

Say your goal. We build the drink.

Type or speak what you need. The engine checks safety first, then designs the best-fit recipe with exact quantities and a live nutrition label.

  1. 1

    Tell us your goal

    “High-protein, low-sugar, post-workout.” Chat or voice, in plain language.

  2. 2

    Safety filter runs first

    Allergen and diet rules (nut, dairy, gluten, diabetic-friendly) apply before anything is ranked.

  3. 3

    Recipe built to your numbers

    Quantities are tuned to your protein, sugar and calorie targets, with calories, macros and glycemic load shown.

  4. 4

    Made fresh, smarter each time

    The kitchen blends it to the recipe card. Ratings and repeat orders refine your next pick.

Protected innovation

The AI system is the heart of PULSE, and we are protecting it. The engine that combines safety filtering, goal-based recipe generation and live nutrition calculation is the subject of our patent application.
